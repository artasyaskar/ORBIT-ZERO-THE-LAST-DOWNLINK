# ORBIT ZERO: THE LAST DOWNLINK
## Judge & Playtester Quick-Start Evaluation Checklist

**Target Platform:** Meta Quest 3 / Quest 3S via Meta Quest Browser (WebXR)  
**Play Mode:** Seated, Hands-First (Zero controllers or keyboard required)  
**Session Duration:** ~4 to 7 minutes per orbital pass  
**Evaluation Standard:** Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 (Gaming Track)

---

## 1. Fast Setup (Under 60 Seconds)

1. Put on your **Meta Quest 3 / Quest 3S**.
2. Open the **Meta Quest Browser**.
3. Navigate to the hosted application URL.
4. Click **`Enter VR Headset / XR Mode`** on the left briefing panel (or the HUD button).
5. Ensure you are comfortably seated in a desk chair. Look straight ahead at the floating Earth globe.
6. *(Optional)* Click **`Recenter Seated VR Viewpoint`** to align the console perfectly with your chest height.

## 3. Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Indus Flood Triage Pass

### Step 1: Scan Highway Levee (`REQ_1` — Sector B)
- **Problem:** Monsoon storm clouds cover the levee corridor.
- **Action:**
  - Verify sensor is set to **RADAR** (blue LED).
  - Aim at **SEC-B** (or press `SCAN SEC-B WITH RADAR`).
  - Evidence Cube docks on the left tray with `[OK] CONFIRMED` floodline telemetry.
- **Processing:**
  - Grab the cube and place it into the **Processing Cradle** on your right (or press `PROCESS SELECTED CUBE`).
  - Haptic hum activates; cube compresses from **3 STR / 4.5s Tx** down to **1 STR / 1.8s Tx** (-6 PWR).

### Step 2: Scan Steel River Crossing (`REQ_2` — Sector C)
- **Problem:** Clear sky slot over the river crossing.
- **Action:**
  - Switch active sensor to **OPTICAL** (press `Switch OPTICAL / RADAR`).
  - Aim at **SEC-C** and scan.
  - Evidence Cube docks with high-resolution visual confirmation.
  - Place in cradle to compress into vectorized bridge approach contours (-6 PWR).

### Step 3: Scan Highland Evacuation Spur (`REQ_3` — Sector E)
- **Problem:** Orographic storm clouds over the ridge spur.
- **Action:**
  - Switch active sensor to **RADAR**.
  - Aim at **SEC-E** and scan.
  - Evidence Cube docks with `[?] PROBABLE` elevation floodline.

### Step 4: Prioritize Downlink Queue
- Place all three processed cubes into the **Downlink Bay** (Slots 1, 2, 3).
- Check the Overhead Telemetry Visor:
  - Queue Duration: **5.4s / 10.0s window** (Safely within the line-of-sight margin!).
  - Power Remaining: **7 PWR** (Sufficient reserves maintained).

### Step 5: Commit Final Downlink
- Press the tactile **COMMIT DOWNLINK** button on the console deck (or debrief panel).
- Watch the live S-band microwave beam transmit data rings into space as the countdown timer ticks.
- Loss of Signal (LOS) occurs: enjoy the comprehensive **Grade S 100/100 Debrief Report** with full evidence provenance and ground rescue impacts!
